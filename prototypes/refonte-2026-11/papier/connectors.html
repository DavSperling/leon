Your conversations with AI assistants.

What gets worked out with an AI assistant often exists nowhere else. Leon keeps it, visible only to its author.

How it works for coding agents Read the documentation
Browser extension
Brings in your conversations from two web-based AI assistants, with your explicit consent. A switch per site and a pause button. Off in private windows; attachments are skipped.
Coding tools
Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Antigravity. One command installs it, and you approve what is sent. Secrets are masked before sending and again on the server.
Your own assistant
Your AI assistant can ask Leon too, over MCP, with your permissions. You authorize it in Leon and can revoke it at any time. Each source it opens in full is logged.
