Before you start.

Something else? Start the free pilot and see what Leon indexes from your own tools.

What counts as an active user?
Anyone who used Leon during the month. Inactive accounts would not be counted, once billing is in place.
Do we need to switch tools?
No, as long as they are among the 42 connectors. Leon connects with OAuth or an API key and syncs changes every 15 minutes.
Who can see what?
Content from a connection is shared with the whole team, except email, Google Drive and Slack, which stay private to whoever connects them. AI conversations, chats and notes are visible only to their author. Read the security page
What happens when Leon doesn’t know?
It says so. Every claim in an answer is cited. Without a source, the answer is “I don’t know”.
Does Leon summarize our documents?
No. It keeps the original text word for word, and each claim cites it with a link to the original.
Can our AI assistant query Leon?
Yes, over MCP. Paste the address into your assistant, authorize it in Leon, revoke it in Settings. It is limited to what you can see.
