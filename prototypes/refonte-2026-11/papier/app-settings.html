Assistants and coding tools

Connect a computer

For people who write code with an AI tool. Open a terminal on the computer and paste this command. Your browser opens so you can approve it.

curl -fsSL https://leonmemory.com/install | sh

One command. It asks which tools to connect and shows what is sent before anything leaves your computer. Works with Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Antigravity.
